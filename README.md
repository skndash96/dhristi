# Dhristi
What if a factory could know a machine was about to fail before production came to a halt?  

DRISHTI is an affordable AI-powered predictive maintenance system that monitors machine health, detects early signs of failure, and helps small factories plan maintenance before breakdowns lead to costly downtime.

# Brief
Small factories often operate ageing machinery without dedicated maintenance teams or expensive monitoring systems. As a result, problems go unnoticed until machines fail, causing production losses and emergency repair costs.  

DRISHTI aims to change this through three capabilities:  

1. MONITOR: Low-cost sensors track machine vibration, temperature, and electrical current.

2. PREDICT: Machine-learning models learn normal operating behaviour and detect unusual patterns that may indicate developing faults, without requiring labelled failure data.

3. ACT: An AI maintenance agent investigates anomalies, suggests troubleshooting steps, checks technician and spare-part availability, and recommends suitable maintenance windows.

The system is designed to work offline for essential monitoring, with factory owners retaining control over all maintenance and shutdown decisions.  

Our initial prototype will focus on an electric motor-driven machine, using Gazebo, ROS 2, Python, and machine-learning techniques.

DRISHTI aims to make predictive maintenance affordable and accessible to small factories, helping them prevent avoidable downtime rather than simply react to failures.
